# CHƯƠNG 1: CÔNG THỨC 4 ĐOẠN BẤT BIẾN CHO MỌI BIỂU ĐỒ

Mọi bài viết Task 1 Band 8.0 đều tuân thủ nghiêm ngặt cấu trúc 4 đoạn (Độ dài tối ưu: 170 – 190 từ):

```text
[Đoạn 1: Introduction] (1 câu - ~20 từ)
Paraphrase lại đề bài bằng cách thay đổi cấu trúc câu và từ đồng nghĩa.
Ví dụ: "The chart shows..." ➔ "The line graph delineates the shifts in..."

[Đoạn 2: Overview] (2 câu - ~40 từ) - PHẦN QUAN TRỌNG NHẤT
Câu 1: Xu hướng tổng quan (Các yếu tố đa phần tăng hay giảm? Quy trình có bao nhiêu bước chính?).
Câu 2: Điểm đột biến / Yếu tố dẫn đầu (Cái nào luôn cao nhất / thấp nhất xuyên suốt thời kỳ?).
*LƯU Ý CỰC KỲ QUAN TRỌNG: Tuyệt đối không đưa số liệu (năm, %, số lượng) vào đoạn này!*

[Đoạn 3: Body Paragraph 1] (3-4 câu - ~60 từ)
Mô tả chi tiết nhóm số liệu thứ nhất (ví dụ: các đối tượng có xu hướng tăng, hoặc giai đoạn nửa đầu thời kỳ). Luôn có số liệu dẫn chứng cụ thể.

[Đoạn 4: Body Paragraph 2] (3-4 câu - ~60 từ)
Mô tả chi tiết nhóm số liệu còn lại và đối chiếu, so sánh với nhóm thứ nhất.
```

## DẠNG 1: LINE GRAPH (BIỂU ĐỒ ĐƯỜNG - BIẾN THIÊN THEO THỜI GIAN)

### 📋 Đề bài mẫu:
> *The line graph below shows the percentage of households with internet access in three European countries from 2005 to 2020.*

### 🎙️ Bài mẫu Band 8.5+ hoàn chỉnh:
```text
The line graph delineates the trajectory of household internet connectivity across three distinct European nations—namely Country A, Country B, and Country C—over a fifteen-year timeframe spanning from 2005 to 2020.

Overall, it is readily apparent that all three countries experienced substantial upward momentum in internet penetration over the period surveyed. Notably, while Country A consistently maintained the highest proportion of connected households throughout the duration, Country C exhibited the most dramatic acceleration, virtually closing the disparity with its counterparts by the end of the timeframe.

Delving into the details, in 2005, Country A commenced with the lion's share of internet access at approximately 45%, eclipsing Country B and Country C, which stood at modest figures of 30% and 15% respectively. Over the subsequent decade, Country A sustained an incremental climb to surpass 70% in 2015, before plateauing gently to conclude the period at just under 85% in 2020.

A contrasting, yet equally pronounced surge was observable in the remaining two nations. Country B mirrored the steady upward trajectory of Country A, progressing reliably to finish at 75% in 2020. Most strikingly, Country C, despite commencing from a negligible base of 15%, underwent an exponential fourfold proliferation after 2010. By the culmination of the surveyed period, Country C’s internet penetration had skyrocketed to reach 78%, thereby overtaking Country B and solidifying widespread digital adoption.
```

#### 💎 Bóc tách từ vựng & Ngữ pháp tạo điểm 8.0:
* `delineates the trajectory of`: phác họa quỹ đạo biến thiên của...
* `incremental climb`: sự leo dốc từ từ, vững chắc.
* `the lion's share of`: phần lớn nhất, áp đảo nhất.
* `plateauing gently`: đi ngang, ổn định nhẹ sau khi tăng.
* `exponential fourfold proliferation`: sự tăng trưởng nhảy vọt gấp bốn lần.

## DẠNG 4: PROCESS DIAGRAM (SƠ ĐỒ QUY TRÌNH SẢN XUẤT & VÒNG ĐỜI)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Quy tắc vàng cho Process:** Tuyệt đối không dùng thì quá khứ! 100% sử dụng **Thì Hiện Tại Đơn (Present Simple)** và tối đa hóa cấu trúc **Bị động (Passive Voice)** (*is heated, are filtered, is packaged*).

### 📋 Đề bài mẫu:
> *The diagram shows the multi-stage process of producing recycled paper from waste paper.*

### 🎙️ Bài mẫu Band 8.5+ hoàn chỉnh:
```text
The flowchart delineates the sequential linear procedure through which post-consumer waste paper is collected, reprocessed, and converted into usable recycled paper products.

Overall, the manufacturing process comprises six distinct stages, commencing with the initial gathering and sorting of discarded paper, proceeding through chemical and mechanical decontamination, and culminating in the compression and rolling of refined pulp into new paper sheets.

At the onset of the operation, waste paper is systematically harvested from municipal recycling repositories and conveyed to a centralized processing facility. Here, the collected material is sorted to eliminate non-recyclable contaminants before being immersed in an aqueous chemical solution. This mixture undergoes rigorous agitation inside a massive vat to disintegrate the paper fibers into a semi-liquid substance known as pulp.

In the subsequent phase, the slurry is subjected to a specialized de-inking process, whereby pressurized air bubbles and detergents are introduced to detach and float residual ink particles to the surface for extraction. Once purified, the pulp is blended with fresh virgin fibers to enhance tensile strength. In the terminal stages, this refined mixture is extruded through heavy heated steel rollers, which simultaneously evacuate excess moisture and flatten the fibers into continuous thin sheets. Finally, the newly manufactured recycled paper is wound onto massive reels, ready for commercial distribution.
```

## DẠNG 5: MAP / PLAN (BẢN ĐỒ QUY HOẠCH ĐÔ THỊ & SỰ BIẾN ĐỔI)

> [!IMPORTANT]

### 🎙️ Bài mẫu Band 8.5+ hoàn chỉnh:
```text
The maps illustrate the infrastructural and commercial redevelopments that materialized in the maritime town of Seaville over a twenty-year period between 1990 and 2010.

Overall, Seaville underwent a comprehensive transformation from a modest fishing-centric settlement into a modernized, leisure-oriented coastal resort. The most salient modifications include the complete eradication of industrial maritime facilities to make way for recreational amenities, alongside a marked expansion of commercial and residential infrastructure.

Examining the shoreline particulars, the traditional fish market and docking berths situated along the western coastline in 1990 were entirely demolished. By 2010, this industrial marine sector had been replaced by a luxurious hotel complex accompanied by a recreational marina. Simultaneously, the adjacent natural sandy beach to the south was commercialized, witnessing the introduction of watersports facilities and seaside cafes.

Further inland, substantial infrastructural enhancements were introduced to facilitate urban connectivity. The disused railway line terminating in the town center in 1990 was dismantled and repurposed into a modern highway, flanked by newly constructed vehicular parking bays. In the north-eastern quadrant of the town, vast tracts of former woodland were cleared to accommodate a sprawling residential subdivision, while the historic town center was pedestrianized and populated with contemporary retail outlets.
```

# CHƯƠNG 3: BỘ TỪ ĐIỂN TỪ VỰNG & NGỮ PHÁP HỌC THUẬT CHO TASK 1

### 1. Động từ & Danh từ mô tả XU HƯỚNG TĂNG:
* **Tăng nhẹ / Từ từ:** *climb steadily, register an incremental increase, tick upward*.
* **Tăng mạnh / Nhảy vọt:** *skyrocket, surge exponentially, witness a dramatic escalation*.
* **Đạt đỉnh:** *reach an apex of, peak at, culminate at a high of*.

### 2. Động từ & Danh từ mô tả XU HƯỚNG GIẢM:
* **Giảm nhẹ / Đều:** *decline moderately, experience an incremental dip, slide downward*.
* **Giảm mạnh / Rơi tự do:** *plummet, slump, witness a steep contraction, dive to an all-time low*.
* **Chạm đáy:** *bottom out at, hit a trough of*.

### 3. Từ vựng biểu thị TỶ LỆ & PHÂN SỐ (Tránh lặp lại dấu %):
* 50% ➔ *exactly a half, accounted for half of the total*.
* 75% ➔ *three-quarters of the surveyed population*.
* 33% ➔ *roughly a third*.
* Chiếm đa số ➔ *constituted the vast majority, commanded the lion's share*.
* Chiếm tỷ lệ nhỏ ➔ *represented a negligible fraction, made up an insignificant minority*.

### 4. Từ vựng biểu thị SỰ XẤP XỈ (Approximations):
* *Approximately, roughly, in the vicinity of, marginally below, just over, hovering around*.
