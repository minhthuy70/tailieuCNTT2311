# PHẦN 1: BỘ KHUÔN ĐÚC PART 1 (HỌC THUỘC LÒNG)

### 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 KHUÔN ĐẶC BIỆT: CHỦ ĐỀ GIA ĐÌNH (FAMILY)
*(Rất hay hỏi ngay sau khi bật máy ghi âm Part 1)*

#### 1. Do you have a large family or a small family? (Gia đình bạn lớn hay nhỏ?)
* **Câu học thuộc:**
  > *"Well, I come from a **nuclear family of four**: my parents, my [younger brother/sister], and myself. Even though our house isn't overly large, it is always full of warmth, and we are a very **tight-knit** family."*
* **Từ vựng hay:** `nuclear family of four` (gia đình hạt nhân 4 người), `tight-knit` (gắn kết, khăng khít).

#### 2. How much time do you spend with your family? (Bạn dành bao nhiêu thời gian cho gia đình?)
* **Câu học thuộc:**
  > *"To be completely honest, since I’m quite occupied with my daily schedule, we usually only gather during dinner time. However, on weekends, we always make it a point to eat together and share stories to **recharge our batteries**."*

#### 3. Who are you closest to in your family? (Bạn thân với ai nhất trong nhà?)
* **Câu học thuộc:**
  > *"Without a shadow of a doubt, it’s my mother. She’s not only a wonderful parent but also a great listener. Whenever I encounter any difficulties in life, she is always the very first person I turn to for advice."*
* **Từ vựng hay:** `without a shadow of a doubt` (chắc chắn không nghi ngờ gì), `turn to for advice` (tìm đến để xin lời khuyên).

#### 4. Do you get along well with your family? (Bạn có hòa thuận với gia đình không?)
* **Câu học thuộc:**
  > *"Definitely yes. Of course, like any other family, we have minor disagreements from time to time. But at the end of the day, we always support each other **through thick and thin**."*
* **Từ vựng hay:** `minor disagreements` (bất đồng nhỏ nhặt), `through thick and thin` (bất kể gian nan, lúc vui cũng như lúc buồn).

### BỘ 4 KHUÔN ĐÚC CHO MỌI CHỦ ĐỀ KHÁC (SỞ THÍCH, THÓI QUEN, QUÁ KHỨ, LỰA CHỌN)

### KHUÔN 1: HỎI VỀ SỞ THÍCH ("Do you like...?")
* **Công thức học thuộc:**
  > *"To be completely honest, I’m a huge fan of **[Tên sở thích]**. I usually do it whenever I have some free time, simply because it helps me relax and recharge my batteries after a long busy day. For instance, just last weekend, I spent a couple of hours doing it and felt really great."*

* **Cách ráp từ:**
  * Giám khảo hỏi: *Do you like reading books?*  
    ➔ Nhét `reading books` vào: *"To be completely honest, I’m a huge fan of reading books. I usually do it whenever I have some free time, simply because it helps me relax and recharge my batteries after a long busy day. For instance, just last weekend, I spent a couple of hours reading my favorite novel and felt really great."*
  * Giám khảo hỏi: *Do you like swimming / traveling / cooking?*  
    ➔ Cứ thế nhét từ vào, nói đúng 3 câu là xong!

---

### KHUÔN 2: HỎI VỀ TẦN SUẤT / THÓI QUEN ("How often do you...?")
* **Công thức học thuộc:**
  > *"Actually, I try to do it as often as possible, probably **[2 or 3 times a week / almost every single day]**. In the past, I used to be quite lazy, but recently I’ve made it a habit because it’s really good for my mental and physical health."*

* **Cách ráp từ:**
  * Giám khảo hỏi: *How often do you exercise / drink coffee / go shopping?*  
    ➔ Ráp ngay: *"Actually, I try to do it as often as possible, probably 2 or 3 times a week. In the past, I used to be quite lazy, but recently I’ve made it a habit because it’s really good for my mental and physical health."*

---

### KHUÔN 3: HỎI SO SÁNH QUÁ KHỨ VỚI HIỆN TẠI ("Did you do X when you were a child?")
* **Công thức học thuộc:**
  > *"Well, there’s a big contrast between now and then. When I was a kid, I was totally into it because I had plenty of leisure time. However, nowadays, since I’m quite occupied with my work and studies, I can only do it once in a while."*

* **Cách ráp từ:**
  * Áp dụng cho mọi câu: *Did you listen to music as a child? / Did you play games? / Did you ride bicycles?*  
    ➔ Nói nguyên văn câu khung trên mà không cần đổi một chữ nào!

---

### KHUÔN 4: HỎI LỰA CHỌN A HOẶC B ("Do you prefer A or B?")
* **Công thức học thuộc:**
  > *"If I had to choose, I’d definitely lean towards **[Phương án chọn]**. The main reason is that it’s much more convenient and cost-effective. On top of that, it gives me a sense of comfort that **[Phương án còn lại]** simply cannot offer."*

* **Cách ráp từ:**
  * Giám khảo: *Do you prefer reading physical books or e-books?*  
    ➔ *"If I had to choose, I’d definitely lean towards physical books. The main reason is that it’s much more convenient and enjoyable. On top of that, it gives me a sense of comfort that reading on screens simply cannot offer."*
  * Giám khảo: *Do you prefer living in a house or a flat? / Traveling alone or in a group?*  
    ➔ Cứ thế nhét A và B vào khung.

# PHẦN 2: SIÊU KHUNG PART 2 - KÉO DÀI 2 PHÚT CHO MỌI ĐỀ
*(CHỈ HỌC ĐÚNG 1 CÂU CHUYỆN NÀY - DÙNG CHO MỌI ĐỀ THI CUE CARD)*

> **CHIẾN THUẬT:** Đề thi bảo tả người, tả địa điểm, tả đồ vật hay tả trải nghiệm?  
> Bạn chỉ cần kể về **"MỘT CHUYẾN ĐI ĐÀ LẠT VỚI BẠN THÂN VÀ CHIẾC ĐIỆN THOẠI"**.  
> - Đề tả **Địa điểm** ➔ Tả Đà Lạt.  
> - Đề tả **Người** ➔ Tả người bạn thân đi cùng (Nam).  
> - Đề tả **Đồ vật / Thiết bị** ➔ Tả chiếc điện thoại dùng để chụp ảnh/dẫn đường trong chuyến đi.  
> - Đề tả **Sự kiện / Kỷ niệm** ➔ Tả chính chuyến đi đó!

### 📝 BÀI NÓI 2 PHÚT HỌC THUỘC LÒNG (KÈM MỐC THỜI GIAN):

```text
[0:00 - 0:30] MỞ ĐẦU (Gặp đề gì cũng mở đầu y hệt câu này):
"I’m going to talk about [Tên chủ đề: Da Lat / my best friend Nam / my smartphone / a memorable trip], which took place about one year ago. At that time, I was feeling extremely stressed out due to my heavy workload, so I decided to take a short trip to Da Lat to clear my head and unwind."

[0:30 - 1:00] BỐI CẢNH & CHI TIẾT CHÍNH:
"We went there by motorbike, and the journey was truly breathtaking with scenic mountain passes and fresh air. During the stay, we spent our days wandering around quiet pine forests, drinking local coffee, and taking hundreds of photos. What impressed me most was the peaceful atmosphere and the hospitable local people, which was completely different from the busy city where I live."

[1:00 - 1:35] CAO TRÀO / SỰ CỐ ĐÁNG NHỚ (Kéo dài thời gian cực tốt):
"However, something unexpected happened on the second day. It started raining heavily and we got completely lost in the mountains without any clear directions. Luckily, thanks to [my friend's quick thinking / my smartphone's Google Maps], we managed to find our way back to a cozy little homestay safely. It was quite scary at first, but looking back, it became an unforgettable memory."

[1:35 - 2:00] KẾT BÀI & CẢM XÚC TỔNG KẾT:
"All in all, that experience meant a great deal to me. It not only helped me recharge my batteries, but also taught me to appreciate the simple things in life. If I have the opportunity in the future, I will definitely do it again without any hesitation."
```

👉 **Cách biến hóa theo từng đề:**
* Nếu đề hỏi **Tả Người:** Nhấn vào *Nam*: *"My best friend Nam is a very calm and reliable person. In this trip, he was the one who..."*
* Nếu đề hỏi **Tả Đồ vật:** Nhấn vào *Chiếc điện thoại*: *"My smartphone was truly a lifesaver because its battery lasted all day and its camera captured amazing memories..."*
* Nếu đề hỏi **Tả Địa điểm:** Nhấn vào *Đà Lạt*: *"Da Lat is a picturesque highland city surrounded by misty valleys..."*

# PHẦN 3: BỘ 4 KHUÔN PHẢN XẠ PART 3 (HỌC ĐỂ CHÉM GIÓ VĨ MÔ)

Part 3 hỏi ý kiến xã hội, rất dễ bị "đứng hình". Hãy học thuộc các câu mở bài và kết bài sau:

### 1. Dạng hỏi: "Điều này có lợi hay có hại?" (Advantages vs Disadvantages)
* **Khung học thuộc:**
  > *"Well, I believe this issue is like a double-edged sword with both pros and cons. On the positive side, it definitely makes people’s lives much easier and more convenient. However, on the flip side, if people become overly dependent on it, it might lead to negative consequences like laziness or health issues. So in short, it depends on how we use it."*

### 2. Dạng hỏi: "Người trẻ và người già khác nhau thế nào?" (Young vs Old)
* **Khung học thuộc:**
  > *"Obviously, there is a noticeable generation gap here. Younger individuals tend to be more tech-savvy, adventurous, and open to new experiences. In stark contrast, the older generation usually prioritizes stability, tradition, and safety. Therefore, their attitudes and choices are quite distinct."*

### 3. Dạng hỏi: "Chính phủ có nên can thiệp/chi tiền không?" (Should Government...?)
* **Khung học thuộc:**
  > *"From my perspective, the authorities should definitely play a leading role in this matter. While individual awareness is important, only the government has sufficient financial resources and legal power to build infrastructure and enforce strict regulations. Without governmental support, it is almost impossible to solve the problem."*

### 4. Dạng hỏi: "Trong tương lai điều này sẽ thay đổi ra sao?" (Future Predictions)
* **Khung học thuộc:**
  > *"It’s hard to say for sure, but I reckon things will change drastically over the next few decades, mainly driven by technological advancements. We will likely see more automation and artificial intelligence taking over routine tasks, which will completely reshape how people live and work."*

# PHẦN 5: KẾ HOẠCH HỌC TẬP 180 PHÚT (3 TIẾNG TRƯỚC GIỜ THI)
